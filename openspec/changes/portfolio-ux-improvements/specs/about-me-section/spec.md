## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Dedicated About Me section
The portfolio SHALL include a dedicated "About Me" section separate from the Overview/Hero section, placed after the hero section in the page flow.

#### Scenario: User navigates to About Me
- **WHEN** a user scrolls past the hero section or clicks "About" in navigation
- **THEN** a dedicated About Me section is displayed with personal narrative content

### Requirement: Personal narrative content
The About Me section SHALL include content that humanizes the developer, including interests, passions, or what drives them professionally.

#### Scenario: User reads About Me section
- **WHEN** a user reads the About Me section
- **THEN** personal touches are visible (e.g., "When I'm not coding, I enjoy...", "I'm passionate about...")

### Requirement: Career narrative
The About Me section SHALL include a brief career journey narrative explaining how the developer arrived at their current expertise.

#### Scenario: User reads About Me section
- **WHEN** a user reads the About Me section
- **THEN** a career narrative is present (e.g., "My journey started at Georgian College, where I discovered...")

### Requirement: Role-seeking information
The About Me section SHALL clearly state what types of roles, projects, or opportunities the developer is actively seeking.

#### Scenario: Recruiter reads About Me section
- **WHEN** a recruiter reads the About Me section
- **THEN** clear information is displayed about what roles/projects the developer is seeking (e.g., "I'm actively seeking opportunities as a Full-Stack Developer...")

### Requirement: Unique value proposition
The About Me section SHALL explain what makes the developer unique or what they specialize in beyond basic skills.

#### Scenario: User reads About Me section
- **WHEN** a user reads the About Me section
- **THEN** unique value proposition is stated (e.g., "I specialize in bridging the gap between frontend excellence and cloud infrastructure...")
